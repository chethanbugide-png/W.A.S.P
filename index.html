The Problem We Solve

120 Hours Is Too Long
to Wait for Clean Water

Conventional BOD₅ testing requires 5 full days of laboratory incubation. During that wait, untreated sewage flows freely into rivers, operators fly blind, and industrial shock loads kill treatment bacteria.

W.A.S.P. replaces the 120-hour lab bottleneck with instant ML predictions from 8 online sensor probes — enabling real-time, closed-loop process control for STPs and CETPs across India's most polluted river basins.

Pipeline

How W.A.S.P. Works

From raw sensor data to actionable treatment decisions in under one second.

01

Data Collection

600 daily records from 5 CPCB STP/CETP corridors. 9 physico-chemical parameters per sample.

02

Preprocessing

Plant-stratified median imputation for 120 missing sensor entries. Z-score standardization.

03

EPLI Indexing

Composite Effluent Pollution Load Index from BOD₅, COD, TSS, and DO deviations.

04

Model Training

OLS, Ridge, Gradient Boosting & Random Forest on 80/20 stratified split (480/120).

05

Predict & Control

Instant BOD₅ prediction, 3-tier classification, shock alerts & automated process actions.
